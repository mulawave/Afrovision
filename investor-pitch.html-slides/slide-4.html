Phase 01
Open Call
Registration opens to all African women aged 18–30. No barriers to entry — novice or experienced, all are welcome to apply.
Phase 02
Selection
Shortlisted participants are evaluated. Diversity of background, raw potential, and resilience indicators are key criteria.
Phase 03
The Gauntlet
Live stress-based challenges streamed on AfroVision. Real-world business scenarios with mounting pressure and public judgment.
Phase 04
The Emergence
5 women are selected as co-founders — receiving resources, visibility, and business infrastructure to launch their ventures.
