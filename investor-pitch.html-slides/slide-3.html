A Program That Builds, Tests & Elevates
Not a popularity contest — a structured pipeline for real business talent
01
Discover
Women aged 18–30 across Africa apply and are selected through a rigorous intake process. Novices and experienced strategists compete side by side.
02
Test
Participants face a series of stress-based challenges simulating real-world business pressure: decision-making, leadership, creative problem-solving, and execution speed.
03
Elevate
5 outstanding women emerge — not as contestants, but as co-founders. The show is their launchpad into Africa's business ecosystem.
This is not about popularity. This is about resilience, raw intelligence, logical intuition and execution under pressure.
