AfroVision · Media Center Plan for review · v1

Surfacing exclusive channel content in the Media Center

Today exclusive movies, series and library items only exist behind a channel tune-in. This plan brings them into the Media Center for entitled members, while keeping every membership, KYC and subscription condition exactly as it is today.

1 · Assumptions I am working from

Entitlement is unchanged

The Media Center is a new surface for content the member is already entitled to. No new access is granted — the same membership + active-subscription check that guards the channel guards every card here.

Tune-in becomes optional, not removed

Number tuning still works. The Media Center is the discovery path; the channel remains the live/linear experience and keeps its own watch gate.

KYC is the visibility key

KYC-unverified non-members see no trace of the exclusive channel — no card, no rail, no title, no search result. KYC-verified non-members see the channel exists, but not its content.

Expiry replaces, never mixes

On expiry the exclusive shelves collapse into a single Renew Membership card. Members do not see a locked grid of their old titles — one clear card, one clear action.

4 · Rules that need holding everywhere

Search and deep links obey the same matrix. State D returns zero exclusive results; a shared link opens the info card (C) or renew card (B) instead of a 404 or an empty player.

No metadata leakage. In C and D the channel's titles, artwork, episode counts and continue-watching progress are never rendered — the premium card is text only.

Downloads and continue-watching follow expiry. Anything cached for an exclusive title stops being playable in B and is presented through the renew card.

Multiple exclusive channels. A member of two channels gets one badged section per channel; the renew and info cards are per channel, named, and never merged into a generic upsell.

Exclusive marking is visual, not a lock. For an active member the badge signals provenance ("from {Channel}"), not restriction — no padlocks on content they can watch.

5 · Decisions locked (from your two review rounds)

These are built into the prototype — open it here.

Placement

Own channel-branded section above public content, plus a single Exclusive toggle chip that filters the tab down to exclusive titles only.

Chip in gated states

Visible in B and C and opens the relevant premium card; hidden entirely in D.

State C actions

Request membership (short bottom-sheet form: reason + optional referral code → “pending approval”) and Learn more (channel about sheet).

Expiring-soon

Dismissible banner at the top of the Media Center from 7 days out. Access unchanged; not repeated on detail or in the player.

Library when expired

Saved exclusive items stay visible, dimmed with a lock; tapping opens the renew sheet rather than the title.

Tune-in when blocked

The dial refuses inline — channel never opens; the message carries the reason and a renew or request action.

Renew card contents

Channel name + mark, expiry date, plan name and price, what is blocked, and the renew CTA.

KYC-unverified

Nothing at all in the Media Center — verification stays in account settings.
